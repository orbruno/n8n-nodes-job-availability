# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Contributors and maintainers commit to providing a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate constructively and assume good intent.
- Focus feedback on the work and its documented requirements.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept correction, take responsibility, and repair harm where possible.
- Protect private information, credentials, and sensitive security reports.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained off-topic hostility.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sharing credentials, private vulnerability details, or personal data in public issues or pull requests.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other public spaces where someone represents this project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Report security vulnerabilities through the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not through a public issue.

Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants whose behavior is inconsistent with this policy. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith is not tolerated.
